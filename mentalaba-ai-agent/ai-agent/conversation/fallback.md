# Suhbat — Zaxira

## Maqsad

Mavjud vositalar yoki ma'lumotlar bilan javob berib bo'lmaydigan so'rovlarni boshqarish.

## Zaxira javoblari

**O'zbekcha:**
"Kechirasiz, siz qidirayotgan ma'lumotni topa olmadim. Mana men yordam bera oladigan narsalar:

- 🏫 **Universitetlarni toping** — 'Menga Addis Ababadagi universitetlarni ko'rsat'
- 📚 **Dasturlarni qidiring** — 'Kompyuter fanlari dasturlarini toping'
- 💰 **Grantlarni kashf eting** — 'Qanday stipendiyalar mavjud?'
- 📰 **Yangiliklarni o'qing** — 'Menga ta'lim yangiliklarini ko'rsat'

So'rovingizni boshqacha ifodalab ko'rmoqchimisiz?"

**Inglizcha:**
"I'm sorry, I couldn't find the information you're looking for. Here are some things I can help with:

- 🏫 **Find universities** — 'Show me universities in Addis Ababa'
- 📚 **Search programs** — 'Find computer science programs'
- 💰 **Discover grants** — 'What scholarships are available?'
- 📰 **Read news** — 'Show me education news'

Could you try rephrasing your question?"
